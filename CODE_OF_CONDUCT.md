# Code of Conduct

Tessera is a project for people who love making music and software. We want
everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background or
identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and give and take feedback about the work, not the
  person.
- Stay on topic and respect people's time.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, or repeatedly derailing discussions.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and
anywhere you represent the project. Report problems privately to the
maintainer through the contact on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/tcrigdon1). The maintainer may remove
comments, lock threads or block people who break these rules.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
